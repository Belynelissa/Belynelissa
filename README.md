# Belyne Lissa Bochere
### Business Intelligence & Performance Analytics | See Beyond Numbers

I help organisations turn business data into clear, useful intelligence—so decision-makers can understand performance, identify risks and opportunities, and make better-informed decisions.

My work has been presented to paying clients. The public examples on this GitHub profile use public, historical or simulated non-client data to respect confidentiality.

## Where I can help

- **Sales and revenue performance** — understand what is selling, where performance is changing, and which areas need attention.
- **Customer behaviour and retention** — examine customer segments, engagement patterns and churn signals.
- **Financial and credit risk** — analyse portfolio patterns, repayment outcomes and potential areas of exposure.
- **Operational and programme performance** — track delivery, budgets, timelines, geographic performance and contractor progress.
- **Management reporting** — turn scattered data into consistent reports and dashboards that support oversight and action.

## What I deliver

- Business intelligence dashboards and KPI reporting
- Data cleaning, analysis and modelling
- SQL-based analysis and reporting
- Performance tracking and management reports
- Customer, sales, financial and operational analysis
- Reporting workflows and automation

## Tools and capabilities

**Power BI · DAX · Power Query · SQL · Python · Excel · Data Modelling · Data Visualisation**

## Ways to work together

I am open to:

- **Employment:** Business Intelligence Analyst, Business Analyst and performance analytics roles, including remote opportunities.
- **Consulting projects:** focused analytical work to answer a defined business question or address a reporting gap.
- **Outsourced BI support:** ongoing or periodic dashboards, analysis and management reporting for organisations that do not need a full-time analyst.

You do not need to have a fully defined analytics brief to start a conversation. If there is a business question you cannot answer confidently, a report that takes too much effort to prepare, or a performance area you need better visibility into, we can explore what the data can tell you.
